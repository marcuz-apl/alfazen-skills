# Hyperframes Motion Graphics

Workflows and best practices for high-framerate, GPU-accelerated motion graphics, generative canvas graphics, and interactive frame sequencing.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Frame Interpolation & Keyframing**: Easing curves, bezier paths, and multi-stage physics animations.
2. **Generative Shaders & Effects**: WebGL/GLSL shader effects, particle engines, and procedural transitions.
3. **Export & Rasterization**: Batch SVG/Canvas frame exports, WebP animation loops, and MP4 conversion pipelines.

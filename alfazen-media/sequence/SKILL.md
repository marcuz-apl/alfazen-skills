# Sequence & Storyboard Orchestration

Workflows for director-level scene composition, storyboarding, pacing rhythm, camera framing, and audio/video sequence assembly.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Beat Sheets & Storyboarding**: Translating narrative scripts into sequenced visual shot lists and timing beats.
2. **Camera Direction & Framing**: Wide, medium, close-up, Dutch angles, pan/tilt/dolly camera movement directions.
3. **Pacing & Scene Transitions**: Match cuts, J-cuts, L-cuts, cross-fades, and rhythmic timing for visual impact.
